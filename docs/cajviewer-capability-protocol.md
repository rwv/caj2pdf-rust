## Session, discovery and document state

At most two fresh distinct containers, one official launcher each, are supported.
Unknown after start consumes its slot. Failure/cleanup expiry stops the next
session; no retry/third launch/alternate profile exists. Counts remain twelve
historical plus at most two new, cumulative maximum fourteen. Admission precedes
every helper/wire/GUI/copy effect. Page/copy attempted, completed, failed,
unverified and remaining work stay separate. First failure survives closing;
publication fallback retains known counts, otherwise null rather than invented 0.
After container start consumes an unknown slot, a collected reported count is
kept separately. Only a complete matching protocol/session receipt with a typed
0/1 count and consistent finite launcher/admission/event ledger can establish
known launch accounting. A malformed zero never erases an admitted unknown slot.
A genuine complete prelaunch failure can establish zero; accounting validation
alone does not establish page/copy or cleanup success. Original receipt failures
and independent closing errors remain separate.

Each session admits an original collector lease, starts its private display/WM,
observes the owned normal window, performs finite discovery, verifies digital.pdf,
acquires physical pages 1–4 and copies page 1, then opens image-only.pdf through
the same app process and acquires/copies its single page. Both sessions repeat
all five pages/two copies. PID peaks/snapshots are not cumulative launch counts.

The live desktop adapter uses bounded public xdotool dispatch and raw X11
observations. Main ownership needs visibility, exact class, normal window type,
live PID/group and stable owner. Open-dialog actions additionally require its
exact class/role/raw marker. Focus/pointer bounds are observed before effects;
generic prompt acceptance is absent. Only the two original input paths may be
typed. Discovery supports at most six fixed opening/ROI-observation/selection
steps, two exact literal safe outcomes each, twelve total discovery actions.
Unknown menus/prompts refuse. Raw bytes and selected branches are retained and
independently checked on the host; the second session must repeat the branches.

Actual controls, coordinates, property semantics and bitmap glyph cells remain
discoveries. Test contracts are fabricated original inputs, not operational
values. Missing document/page/count/extent observations refuse. Source size/SHA,
physical zero-based index, raw UI-label observation, owner, physical box/count
and navigation generation are separate. An arbitrary hash, title, dialog or
VERIFIED boolean cannot substitute for raw identity and original content proof.

## Complete-page and ordinary-copy gates

The implemented origin is complete-page capture. Each exact 1600×1200 P6 frame
is independently decoded. A frozen page rectangle must fit inside the owned
window/display with an observable exterior ring. Every original pixel, all four
boundary rings and asymmetric corners match exactly. Blank pages additionally
require verified source/page/navigation; white pixels alone fail. Equivalent
digital/negative or normal/large rasters require their separate raw identities.
Normal grids are 1026×769, rotated 769×1026; source boxes are 256.5×192.25 points
and 2052×1538 for the large control. Exact rational pixels/point and raw/decoded
dimensions, lengths and hashes remain distinct. No crop search, rescaling,
interpolation, alignment or tolerance widening exists. Unsupported layouts and
incomplete captures remain located failures. Native/print origins are scoped
UNAVAILABLE pending their separate settings/rasterizer and resource amendment.

The original X11 client uses only the private Unix display. Before each copy it
installs and reads a unique sentinel through actual selection conversion. After
ordinary-copy dispatch it requires a real server SelectionClear event, TARGETS,
new application-owned TIMESTAMP revision tied to the event, complete payload
and stable final owner/revision. Raw event, sentinel pin, target/encoding and
transfer/text proof are checked again on the host. Synthetic clear events refuse.
A persistent manager is scoped UNAVAILABLE until a demonstrated content revision
mechanism is frozen; TIMESTAMP alone cannot prove that manager revision.
Delays/changed bytes/stale sentinel/timeout never prove freshness or empty copy.

Direct and INCR transfers retain exact type, bounded targets/pieces/bytes and a
zero-length terminal. Every nested call inherits the transaction deadline and
late completion refuses. Complete empty, absent, stale and interrupted outcomes
are distinct. Strict code points/order/coverage preserve spaces/punctuation with
no normalization/reflow. Digital/negative results remain separate; ordinary-copy
origin is recorded, native Unicode/OCR origin UNVERIFIED.
